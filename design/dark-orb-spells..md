# Dark Orb Spell Roster

This document organizes a large spell roster for a Dragonlance/Forgotten Realms-inspired DnD game using verified AD&D 2e spell references as the baseline while extending them into a game-design format with impact categories such as HP, TM, MP, Magic Resistance, Armor Class, and Movement.[web:2][web:6][web:41][web:48] The schools and impact language below are tuned for a broader custom spellbook with variants rather than a strict reproduction of only one published ruleset.[web:41][web:48]

## Notes

- **Mage** maps to wizard-style arcane casting.[web:2]
- **Priest** maps to cleric/specialty-priest style divine casting.[web:6]
- **Druid** is treated as a nature-priest branch with overlapping divine spell access.[web:6]
- **Paladin** is assigned a narrower priest-like support/protection subset for game design use, but in AD&D 2e paladins only begin casting from character level 9 and only from the Combat, Divination, Healing, and Protection priest spheres.[web:34]
- **Spell Level** is spell-circle level, not required character level.[web:6][web:2]
- **Impact** shows what the spell changes on the target or battlefield: HP, TM, MP, Magic Resistance, Armor Class, or Movement.[web:2][web:6]
- **TM** is a custom game term for turn meter; for rooted, slowed, panicked, paralyzed, or tempo-suppressing effects, the source spell is mapped into TM or Movement impact as a design interpretation.[web:21][web:23][web:25]
- **MP** leech and anti-caster drain are included as Dark Orb variant hooks because baseline AD&D 2e references support anti-mage control, necromantic drain themes, and silence/feeblemind disruption, but do not universally use modern mana-point terminology.[web:2][web:48]
- **Afterburn** means a lingering damage-over-time rider or a persistent hazard zone, including acid continuation, poison cloud persistence, heat escalation, or active barriers.[web:2][web:25]

## Spell schools

Dark Orb can use six high-level schools that sit comfortably on top of AD&D-style school logic, where wizard magic is already grouped into schools such as Abjuration, Alteration, Conjuration/Summoning, Divination, Illusion/Phantasm, Invocation/Evocation, and Necromancy.[web:41][web:48] The six schools below are deliberately broader and more game-facing so variant spells can live in the same family while keeping strong fantasy identity.[web:41][web:48]

| School | Focus | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Aegis | Wards, protection, resistance | Aegis magic focuses on shields, dispels, sanctuaries, anti-magic, armor reinforcement, and survival tools that improve Armor Class, saves, Magic Resistance, or damage mitigation.[web:41][web:48] |
| Stormcraft | Raw elemental force | Stormcraft governs direct elemental offense such as fire, lightning, frost, explosive blasts, and battlefield hazards; it is the main home for HP-damage nukes and electrified variants.[web:41][web:48] |
| Verdancy | Nature and natural elements | Verdancy channels roots, beasts, wind, stone, plants, insects, healing herbs, and primal weather, making it the school for druids, natural elemental power, movement control, and terrain magic.[web:6][web:41] |
| Umbramancy | Dark magic, death, undead, shadow | Umbramancy covers death magic, undead animation, shadows, curses, fear, life-drain, anti-caster leeching, soul pressure, and sinister battlefield control.[web:41][web:48] |
| Mirage | Illusion, stealth, deception | Mirage handles invisibility, mirror images, blurs, fear-phantasms, false positioning, sensory manipulation, and target confusion.[web:41][web:48] |
| Dominion | Command, blessing, mind and morale | Dominion magic governs commands, divine words, blessings, prayer, courage, panic, turn-meter acceleration, and status control affecting discipline, will, and battle momentum.[web:6][web:47] |

## Variant design rules

The spellbook is intentionally vast, so each baseline spell can spawn variants without breaking its fantasy role.[web:2][web:41] For example, Lightning Bolt can have a Stormcraft variant that adds **Electrocute** for HP damage plus TM suppression, while Umbramancy can add MP leech variants for anti-mage play and Dominion can add battle-cries or war-cries that boost ally TM and morale.[web:2][web:47][web:48]

## Impact mapping

These impact labels are intended to support your combat systems directly: **HP** for damage and healing, **TM** for initiative or turn-meter acceleration and suppression, **MP** for anti-caster drains and leeching, **Magic Resistance** for anti-magic protection or magical vulnerability, **Armor Class** for physical survivability, and **Movement** for roots, slows, teleports, displacement, and terrain denial.[web:2][web:6][web:46][web:47] This keeps defensive spells and offensive control spells in the same framework without losing fantasy flavor.[web:46][web:47]

## Variant hooks for the vast spellbook

A large spellbook works best when each baseline spell can have controlled branches rather than unrelated one-offs.[web:41][web:48] Good examples are Lightning Bolt into Electrocute or Chain Lightning variants, Barkskin into Thornskin or Ironbark variants, Web into Venom Web or Shadow Web variants, and Umbramancy lines such as Mind Siphon and Mana Leak for MP drain against hostile casters.[web:2][web:46][web:48]
