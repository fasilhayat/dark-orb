# BattleArena — Monsters & Opponents

> Every monster stat block uses the same AD&D-inspired system: **lower AC is better**, attack rolls use `d20 + AttackBonus ≥ StrikeRating - targetAC`.  
> **Level** indicates the equivalent character level the monster is balanced against.  
> Monsters come in three tiers: **Lesser** (weaker variant), **Normal** (unprefixed), **Greater** (elite variant).
