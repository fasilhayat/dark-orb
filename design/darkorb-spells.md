# Dark Orb Spell Roster

This document organizes a long-form spell roster for a Dragonlance/Forgotten Realms-inspired DnD game using verified AD&D 2e spell sources and searchable spell references.[web:2][web:6][web:15][web:25] It is designed as a game-design reference rather than a claim to reproduce every spell from every TSR compendium volume.[web:12][web:15]

## Notes

- **Mage** maps to wizard-style arcane casting.[web:2]
- **Priest** maps to cleric/specialty-priest style divine casting.[web:6]
- **Druid** is treated as a nature-priest branch with overlapping divine spell access.[web:6]
- **Paladin** is assigned a narrower priest-like support/protection subset for game design use, but in AD&D 2e paladins only begin casting priest spells from 9th level and only from the Combat, Divination, Healing, and Protection spheres.[web:34]
- **Spell Level** in the tables below is the spell-circle level from the AD&D 2e priest or wizard list, not the character level required to unlock it.[web:6][web:2]
- **Afterburn** means a lingering damage-over-time rider such as burning, acid continuation, poison cloud persistence, or hazard persistence; when the original source does not explicitly state a burn rider, the field is marked conservatively.[web:2][web:25]
- **Damage Type** uses game-design labels such as Fire, Cold, Lightning, Acid, Poison, Necrotic, Radiant, Physical, Force, or Control/None for utility spells.[web:2][web:6][web:25]


## Level restrictions

Spell-circle levels are now included in every table, and those levels come from the AD&D 2e priest and wizard spell lists used as the baseline here.[web:6][web:2] For paladins specifically, access is further restricted because they only begin casting from character level 9 and only from the Combat, Divination, Healing, and Protection priest spheres.[web:34]

## Design hooks for Dark Orb

Several verified spells naturally map to the mechanics you described, especially **rooting**, **turn-meter suppression**, and **persistent hazard fields**.[web:23][web:25][web:21] Entangle and Web are the main rooted-zone spells, while Grease is the slippery oil spell, and Blade Barrier is a persistent physical hazard wall that fits divine battlefield control.[web:18][web:23][web:25][web:21]

For system adaptation, Slow can become turn-meter reduction or tick suppression, Vampiric Touch can become HP leech, and an original dark-arcane mana leech line can be added as a custom Mage family inspired by the anti-caster role of Feeblemind, Silence, and drain-style magic rather than pretending it is baseline canon terminology.[web:2][web:6]
