# NPC Reference

## Quick Reference

| Name | Race | Class | Lvl | Role |
|------|------|-------|:---:|------|
| **Old Man Kael** | Human | Priest | 8 | Quest Giver — *A blind seer who speaks in riddles. He knows the location of the Sun's Wrath and will trade the knowledge for a vial of dragon's blood.* |
| **Greta Ironhand** | Dwarf | Fighter | 12 | Merchant — *A dwarf smith who forged weapons for three kings. She keeps the Soul Reaver hidden beneath her forge, waiting for a worthy champion. She buys and sells all weapons and armor.* |
| **Shadowmere** | Elf | Rogue | 10 | Quest Giver — *The leader of the Nightblades guild. She offers membership to those who prove their worth by retrieving the Shadow Sting from the Crypt of Whispers.* |
| **Korg Stonefist** | Orc | Barbarian | 15 | Hostile — *A wandering orc berserker who challenges all who cross his path. Wields a massive maul and wears cursed plate that feeds on his pain.* |
| **Merchant Vex** | Kobold | Rogue | 6 | Merchant — *A kobold trader with a cart full of "authentic" artifacts. Most are fakes, but occasionally she comes across a real treasure. She sells rings, amulets, and girdles.* |
| **High Priestess Luna** | Human | Priest | 14 | Quest Giver — *The head of the Moon temple, devoted to Lunara. She bestows the Amulet of the Archon upon those who complete the pilgrimage to the Moonlit Peak. Wields the Great Mace.* |
| **Graveworm** | Undead | Fighter | 9 | Hostile — *An undead warlord who commands a legion of skeletons in the Bone Fields. He carries Frostbite, the blade that killed him centuries ago.* |
| **Lysander the Bard** | Gladefolk | Bard | 7 | Quest Giver — *A Gladefolk bard who knows every legend, song, and secret in the realm. He can reveal the location of any legendary item for a price.* |
| **Infernal Commander Zoth** | Demon | Knight | 18 | Hostile (Boss) — *A demon lord commanding the legions of the Fire Pits. He wields the Stormbringer lance and rides a nightmare steed. The final boss of the Burning Plains.* |
| **Elder Treant** | Elf | Druid | 20 | Quest Giver — *An ancient treant awakened by the druids of the Deepwood. He grants the Dragon Scale Mail to those who prove they can protect the forest.* |
